04 — What This Tells Us

Dubai property does not
run on oil money.

01
No significant Granger causality in any segment. All p-values sit above 5%. Past Brent prices do not significantly predict Dubai residential prices in a two-variable VAR over this window.
02
Dubai's GDP doesn't depend on oil. The city runs on trade, logistics, tourism, and finance. That's Abu Dhabi. Brent price swings have no direct channel into Dubai's fiscal position or public employment.
03
The 2022–2024 boom was not an oil story. The largest price run in DLD history came from post-Ukraine migration and Golden Visa expansion. None of that correlates with Brent crude.
04
Villas are the most interesting result. p = 0.084 at a 3-month lag is not noise. If oil has any path into Dubai property, it runs through GCC nationals buying in the luxury segment. FEVD caps that at 2.1%.
05
The 2022–2024 structural break reduces ADF power. The price surge was so sharp and non-oil-driven that ADF tests become inconclusive on first differences. KPSS confirms log-differences are stationary — both tests together justify the VAR transformation.
